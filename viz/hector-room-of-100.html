    About the data

    HBSC international open-access files for 2005/06, 2009/10 and 2013/14, about 185,000–195,000 students per year. The rating is self-reported and relative to classmates. It is not a grade or test score. Shares are unweighted and rounded so each room adds up to 100. Each room is a different group of students, so the page shows an association, not cause and effect. Correlations are rank (Spearman) correlations between screen-time level and rating. With this many students, even −0.05 is statistically significant (p < 0.001), but the size of the link is small. “All screens” adds TV, computer and game hours together.
