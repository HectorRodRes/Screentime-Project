              Phone calls are voice calls only. ATUS does not directly measure smartphone or social media scrolling, so screen time is undercounted.

              Years are 3-year averages (2004–06, 2014–16, 2023–25). Generation differences within one year partly reflect age and life stage.

              People coded 85 and older are left out in every period, because that group always includes people born before 1928 (before the Silent Generation). Ages 80–84 are included.

              Base hours are figured from each generation's own averages. Total hours = the full 24-hour day. Waking hours = 24 hours minus average sleep. Leisure hours = 24 hours minus average sleep and average work; it still includes eating, chores, caregiving, commuting, and school.

              Sources: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, American Time Use Survey, activity summary file 2003–25; Pew Research Center generation definitions; U.S. Census Bureau population estimates (July 1 resident population).
